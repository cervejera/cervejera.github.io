Enaex

Interactive corporate presentation, with dashboards linked to Excel

Client project for Enaex, a company in the explosives and mining sector. A click-through presentation on strategy and financial results, with 28 charts fed by a single spreadsheet.

client project · enaex · 2026 · powerpoint and excel

the numbers in the charts were replaced with fictional values. the design and structure are the originals.

problem

A results presentation that had to be navigated live and updated every year without rebuilding the charts.

my role

Client project: I structured the spreadsheet, built the 28 charts and designed the whole presentation.

result

One data source feeds every chart. A new year is one more row, without touching PowerPoint.

The problem

The presentation had to work live, with the presenter jumping to any topic on the spot, and the charts had to be easy to update every year without rebuilding anything.

How it was built

  1. 01

    A single data source

    Every yearly series lives in a DATA tab. Each slide's tab reads from it with formulas, and nobody types numbers anywhere else.

  2. 02

    A new year without rebuilding charts

    Adding a new year takes one more row in DATA, marked as LTM. The red bar for the latest period moves on its own.

  3. 03

    Built-in checks

    Check columns recalculate the margins. Plus a color code: blue is a typed value, black is a formula, green is a reference to another tab, and yellow is a discrepancy found in the deck, pending confirmation with the client.

  4. 04

    Charts linked to PowerPoint

    Each chart is sized in Excel at its final slide size and pasted as a link. Automatic updating is turned off so the link warning never pops up in front of the audience.

  5. 05

    Click-through navigation

    A clickable table of contents and back, next and home buttons on every slide, for presenting in kiosk mode.
