ConCafé

Visual identity for a specialty coffee event

Key visual for ConCafé 2026, a gathering of the people who grow, brew and drink the coffee of the Serra de Apucarana region.

design · sebrae/pr · 2026

more Sebrae/PR pieces on Behance ↗

problem

A specialty coffee event that needed its own identity, tied to the region.

my role

I created the key visual: concept, logo, colors, typography and applications.

result

Identity applied to the big screen, printed pieces and event variations in 2026.

The concept

The period in "Con." signals a word that continues: a connection completed by coffee. The bean takes the place of the "O" and the leaf plays the role of the accent, bringing the origin into the brand. The mountains in the pieces are the real skyline of Apucarana, and the dotted rows recreate coffee planted along contour lines.
