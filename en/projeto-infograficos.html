Infographics

Sebrae/PR surveys in a format you can read at a glance

Surveys of entrepreneurs and consumers in the state of Paraná, turned into infographics for the press and internal communication. It's the part of my work where design and data meet.

design · sebrae/pr · 2023 to 2026

problem

Surveys arrive as tables: percentages, scores and counts that are hard to read at a glance.

my role

I pick the right chart for each data point and design the piece within Sebrae/PR's identity.

result

Pieces used by the press and internal communication, from 2023 to 2026.

The challenge

Every survey arrives as a table: percentages, 0 to 10 scores, counts by region. The job is to choose the form that makes the answer obvious without reading the number, and to keep Sebrae's identity in all of them.

How I choose the chart

  1. 01

    Part of a whole

    Donut or pie when there are only a few slices, like types of bank account or company size.

  2. 02

    Frequency in ranges

    Stacked bars, like how many times a month people order food.

  3. 03

    Scores and ratings

    Cards with a big number, comparable at a glance.

  4. 04

    Data by region

    A map of Paraná with the number in each region.

  5. 05

    Ranking

    A podium or sorted bars, when the position matters more than the value.
